Complication No. 8

A scroll-led exploration of a high-complication Swiss movement. Each component reveals itself, then assembles into a singular expression of mechanical grace.

Main Plate

Hand-finished German silver establishes the movement's geometry. The base is brushed in radial grain and beveled by hand to catch light with quiet dignity.

Barrel & Bridge

Twin barrels accumulate reserve for extended autonomy, secured by an arched bridge whose polished flanks frame every contour with architectural precision.

Gear Train

A calibrated transmission route delivers torque through jewel-mounted pivots, balancing efficiency and stability to maintain an unwavering cadence.

Escapement

The anchor and wheel negotiate each impulse in measured intervals, converting stored force into regulated motion—traditional engineering, refined for contemporary expectations.

Balance Assembly

A free-sprung balance with mirror-polished screws breathes at the core. Its poise and symmetry are tuned to preserve accuracy under daily shifts in position.

Dial & Hands

Fired enamel and blued-steel hands complete the face. What begins as six singular components resolves into one coherent timepiece—reserved, modern, and unmistakably Swiss.
