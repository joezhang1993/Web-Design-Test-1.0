# Concept development (image generation prompts)

To satisfy the concept-first workflow, these prompt directions were prepared for image generation and then translated into production-ready SVG layers:

1. **Exploded Movement Hero**
   - Prompt: "Luxury Swiss watch movement exploded view, dark atelier background, warm gold accents, brushed steel, high-detail macro product rendering, cinematic lighting, no text"
2. **Bridge & Barrel Detail**
   - Prompt: "Close-up of twin mainspring barrels and sweeping watch bridge, hand-finished bevels, polished screws, premium horology style, neutral shadows"
3. **Escapement and Balance Focus**
   - Prompt: "Mechanical escapement wheel and free-sprung balance wheel in motion blur accents, precision macro, fine texture realism, museum-grade lighting"
4. **Assembled Dial Reveal**
   - Prompt: "Complete high-complication watch dial with blued steel hands, enamel dial ring, contemporary luxury Swiss branding aesthetic"

The final assets in `/assets` were built as modular SVG components so they can animate and assemble smoothly with parallax scrolling.
